## Root Controller Series

The Root controller series is the core hardware of the RyzoBee ecosystem, connecting, controlling, and managing peripheral modules. It serves as the electronic brain of 3D-printed creations, desktop devices, interactive models, and maker projects, providing lighting control, motor driving, sensor readings, displays, networking, and AI interaction.

## Yoke Accessories

Yoke accessories are modular expansion components designed around Root controllers, covering common functions such as lighting, motors, sensors, displays, audio, and cables. Standardized interfaces and accessory specifications simplify adding functions, reduce complex wiring and driver integration, and make projects clearer, more reliable, and easier to reproduce.
